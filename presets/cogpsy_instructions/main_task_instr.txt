Are you ready to start the game?

In the actual game:

1. You will not see the reward bar - but the rules for earning money remain the same, and you will receive feedback about your reward after every block.

2. Please focus your eyes on the centre of the radioactive source and do not follow the beams with your eyes. This is to minimise eye-movement artefacts in the MEG data.

3. You will hear tones through your headphones while you play the game. These tones are completely unrelated to the task - you can ignore them and focus on catching the beams.

Press any key to continue.
